# Design System

## Colors (CSS Variables)
- --bg: app background
- --surface: card/surface background
- --text: primary text
- --muted: secondary text
- --border: neutral borders
- --primary: brand accent blue
- --primary-strong: primary hover/gradient stop

## Radius & Shadows
- --radius-sm: 10px
- --radius-md: 16px
- --radius-lg: 24px
- --shadow: elevated card shadow

## Typography Scale
- H1: clamp(1.7rem, 3vw, 2.55rem)
- H2/H3: compact and readable with 1.2 line-height
- Body: 1rem baseline with 1.6 line-height

## Buttons
- .btn: standard action
- .btn.primary: high-emphasis CTA
- .btn.ghost: low-emphasis secondary action
- .icon-btn: compact utility button

## Components
- Topbar (sticky)
- Breadcrumbs
- Cards (content and KPI variants)
- Search form
- Toolbar controls
- Footer link grid

## Interaction Patterns
- Hover: slight upward movement + shadow
- Reveal: staggered entrance animation
- Theme and language-direction toggles persisted in local storage
